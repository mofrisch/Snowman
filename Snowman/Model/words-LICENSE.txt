Snowman selection: four to ten ASCII letters and an independent English
headword sense. The filter checks the original dictionary spelling before
converting to lowercase. It excludes abbreviations, all-caps and mixed-case
headwords, and senses marked as rare, obsolete, dialectal or nonstandard.
Title-case words and spellings also used as inflections require matching
Open English WordNet entries. If the spelling also matches an uppercase
abbreviation, the filter requires an independent lowercase WordNet entry
or a lowercase function-word sense in Wiktionary.

The score is the subtitle count of the headword plus counts of inflections
with a single dictionary target and no independent headword retained by the
filter. Frequency ties are broken alphabetically. The 30,000 highest scoring
words are stored in uppercase alphabetical order.
